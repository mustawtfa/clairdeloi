Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Clair De Loi, membership, and how to get involved.

Clair De Loi is an international, youth-led legal community that brings together young people interested in law, justice, and related fields to learn, connect, research, and create together.
Anyone interested in law, justice, and legal issues can join our community. You do not need to be a law student or have previous legal experience to become a member.
Members can participate in workshops, case studies, legal writing projects, discussions, research activities, social responsibility projects, and other community initiatives.
Yes. Depending on the available positions, members may have the opportunity to apply for teams such as Newsletters, Research, Design, or Chapters.
You can apply to establish or represent a Clair De Loi Chapter in your region. Applications are reviewed based on the applicant's motivation, commitment, and ability to develop the local community.
Our community activities and events are free to attend. Specific events may have different participation requirements, which will be announced beforehand.
You can follow our official social media channels and join our community to receive updates about upcoming events, opportunities, applications, and projects.
We are open to collaborations with students, academics, professionals, organizations, and youth communities working in relevant fields. You can contact us through our official communication channels to discuss potential collaborations.
